How This Appeal Works

The appeal challenges the County Planning Commission (CPC)’s July 1, 2026 Notice of Decision (NOD) that upheld the Zoning Administrator (ZA)’s approval of the conditional use permit (CUP) for the Islamic Center at 9600 2nd Street NW. However, the arguments in this appeal are not limited to the words on the Notice of Decision (NOD) alone.

The appeal draws from the full record — including the certified hearing transcript, hearing testimony, the application, the approved site plan, staff statements, traffic materials, emails between the County and the Appeal Coalition, County rules and procedures, evidence in the County record, and all exhibits filed with the Board of County Commissioners (BCC). Together, these show whether specific Notice of Decision (NOD) findings and conditions were met at the Zoning Administrator (ZA) level and the County Planning Commission (CPC) / Board of Adjustment (BOA) level, whether the Notice of Decision (NOD) itself contains errors, and whether those findings were erroneous, an abuse of discretion, or unsupported by evidence.

In plain terms: the Commission’s decision is being challenged not just on what the Notice of Decision (NOD) says, but on whether the evidence in the record actually supports what the Commission decided.

Appeal Brief — One-Page Summary

The appeal challenges the approval of a high-volume mosque at 9600 2nd Street NW, citing lack of project-specific traffic analysis, procedural violations, and inadequate evidence supporting safety and neighborhood impact findings. Key issues include deferral of traffic studies to permit stage, inconsistent parking and attendance data, and failure to address regional corridor capacity, with requests for remand, comprehensive traffic review, and policy reforms.
